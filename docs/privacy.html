Privacy Policy — IamRushi Quiz Uploader

Last updated: 27 October 2026

IamRushi Quiz Uploader is a personal automation used only by the owner of the IamRushi YouTube channel. It requests a single Google permission, youtube.upload, to upload quiz videos the owner creates to the owner's own channel.

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people.
  • It does not read any other data from the Google account.
  • The authorization token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private automation settings and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: through the About page of the IamRushi YouTube channel.
