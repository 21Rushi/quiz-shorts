## Your 10 minutes a day (this is what gets the channel monetised)
YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule demonetises channels that only post near-identical templated videos. The automation does the volume; these human touches make it a real channel:

1. **Pin a comment** on each new Short: "Kitne logon ne sahi jawab diya? 👇" — comments are the strongest signal for quiz Shorts.
2. **Reply to the first 10–15 comments** within the first hour.
3. **Check Studio every 3 days**: which theme got the best "viewed vs swiped away"? Do more of that in the next week's `schedule.json`.
4. **Once a week, record a 20–30 s face/voice Short** ("Last week's hardest question — only 12% got it!"). Original voice + commentary is what YouTube wants to see in a monetisation review.
5. **Post one Community poll per week** with a bonus question (unlocks after you have enough subscribers).
